Questions

Will my staff notice anything day to day?

No. They keep using Outlook as normal. The backup runs in the background.

Doesn't Microsoft already back up my email?

Microsoft keeps its service running, but when Microsoft is down, its copies are down with it. This keeps a copy somewhere else.

Will customers see a different address?

No. Your team sends and receives from their usual addresses.

How long does setup take?

One to two weeks, with no interruption to your email.
